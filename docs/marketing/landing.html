Why NIKI

Multi-agent, not monolithic
Planning, coding, testing, review are separate agents with their own models — each does one job well.
Hermetic by default
Your working tree is never mutated mid-run. Output is a real git branch.
Independent by design
Agents share no context — only typed artifacts. No confirmation bias, no context drift.
BYOK & provider-mixing
Bring your own keys. A strong reasoner for Planner/Reviewer, a cheap model for Tester.
Fully auditable
report.md + artifacts/*.json capture what every agent decided and why.
Reviewer-driven revisions
The Reviewer loops back to the Coder up to max_revision_rounds before completion.

What NIKI is NOT

  • ✗ Not a replacement for your judgment — you still review the diff.
  • ✗ Not a single all-knowing agent — independence is the point, at the cost of one long context.
  • ✗ No training on your code, no telemetry, no hosted service required.
  • ✗ The worktree backend runs on your host — use the container backend for untrusted tasks.
  • ✗ [session]/[compaction]/[mcp]/[permissions] are parsed but not yet wired (NIKI says so at load).
